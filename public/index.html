Server rendering uses your Railway memory, CPU, and persistent storage. 1080p is best rendered on your own computer.
Ready to render your changes into a new video.

On your computer: install Node.js 20.18+ and FFmpeg, then run node local-render.mjs zaininsta-edit.json from the project folder. This downloads your uploaded assets and renders locally. Keep your website online during the download.

After local rendering, upload the MP4 here; it will be selected for your Reel. Local CPU/RAM usage is not charged to Railway, but the uploaded MP4 uses Railway storage and bandwidth.
